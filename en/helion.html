Who it is for

For teams with a proven service
that needs to become a clear package.

Helion is for B2B service firms in IT, cyber, SAP/ERP, industrial engineering and specialist process consulting: 5–40 people in the DACH region, with a delivery track record. Your service is new or current and should be sold to end clients as a fixed-price package, but its scope, price, outcomes or sales materials are not yet clear enough.

The goal

An offer that makes sense without an explanation.

Your clients can see at a glance what is included, what is not and what it costs. They can pass the offer on internally and make a clear decision.

How we work

A clear process, entirely in writing.

01 · Enquiry

You answer seven short questions about your service, clients and schedule. We confirm in writing whether Helion suits your project.

02 · Production

Once we have the signed order confirmation, full payment and complete start materials, we produce your fixed-price offer and sales copy.

03 · Delivery

You receive all materials in an organised folder. One written revision against the agreed checklist is included.

Helion 72

What you receive.

  • A 1–3 page fixed-price offer that is clear enough for a yes-or-no decision
  • Positioning against the competitors you name, based only on public information
  • One core offer with up to two extensions, each with a clear out-of-scope list
  • Pricing logic with all assumptions marked
  • Ready-to-use landing page copy
  • Ten outreach messages for potential buyers
  • A one-page guide to the next sales step
  • A sources and assumptions sheet, with all materials in an organised folder

Not included: sales calls with procurement, website implementation, invented case studies or a guarantee that the deal will close. A fixed pitch date is not required.

When Helion is not a match

So both sides have clarity quickly.

Helion is not intended for coaches, courses or personal brands. It is also not a match for firms without a delivery track record, already mature packages or services that will continue to be sold only by the day. The process is deliberately written; if a personal introductory call is essential for you, Helion is not the right offer.

Short answers

The key questions.

Who is Helion for?

B2B service firms in IT, cyber, SAP/ERP, industry and specialist process consulting: 5–40 people in the DACH region, with a delivery track record and a service that should be sold as a fixed-price package.

Do I need a fixed pitch date?

No. What matters is that you want to offer your service clearly and soon. A specific pitch date is not required.

How does the enquiry work?

You answer seven short questions. We review whether Helion suits your project and reply in writing. No introductory meeting is needed.

What does the fixed price cover?

CHF 4,900 covers the fixed-price offer, sales copy and the other materials listed above. You are buying a clearly defined result, not hours.

How do I pay?

After review, you receive a written order confirmation. The paid order is formed only when you return it signed. You then pay CHF 4,900 in full through the existing Stripe link.

Are there workshops or meetings?

No. The enquiry, production, delivery and one revision all happen in writing, keeping the process quick and easy to follow.

Can we speak first?

Helion is deliberately designed as a written process. If a personal introductory call is essential for you, this offer is unlikely to suit your preferred way of working.
