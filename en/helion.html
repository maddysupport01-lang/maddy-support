The paper

The other has service fields, certificates and an invitation to a conversation.

Helion 72

What is on the table after 72 hours.

  • A fixed-price offer on 1–3 pages, clear enough for procurement and an internal decision
  • A distinction against the competitors you name, based on publicly available information
  • A clear offer architecture: core work, at most two extensions, and a cleanly defined out-of-scope
  • A readable price logic with marked assumptions
  • Wording for the sell-side
  • Ten fitting cover notes for the buy-side
  • A compact playbook for the next pitch
  • Sources, assumptions and the full handover file

Not included: a promised pitch win, conversations with procurement, technical website work, or invented references.

Short answers

No detours.

Who exactly?

Helion 72 is for specialist firms with a delivery record and a concrete pitch — upcoming or just lost. IT, cyber, SAP/ERP, industrial engineering and specialised process advisory. DACH and international, typically 5–40 people.

Is that not expensive?

CHF 4,900 is the fixed price for a full Helion 72 mandate, not for consulting hours. On a commercially relevant pitch the real question is not what a document costs — it is what an unclear document can cost.

How do I pay?

After review and written acceptance you receive the payment link. The 72-hour window starts on receipt of payment.

Can I not do this myself?

Of course. If you have the time and distance to reduce your existing offer to a clear yes/no for procurement, you do not need Helion 72.

I have no time for workshops.

Good. Neither do we. The mandate is reviewed in writing, work starts after payment, and 72 hours later the pack is there. One written revision round is included.

Does AI not already do this?

AI can produce text. Helion 72 does not turn that into a longer document. It builds a buyable offer: name, scope, out-of-scope, price and a basis for decision.

Does Helion work for my competitor?

Per pitch we work on one side only. During the agreed window we do not take a mandate from a direct competitor in the same process.

Can we just hop on a call?

Helion 72 is built to stay in writing. Review, engagement, work and handover run without a meeting. If a point must be cleared for a solid result, we ask in writing.
