Clarity before the first mail

Short answers.

What exactly do you review?

The publicly visible statements of a target company — founding year, location, team size, service claims. We show what matches, what conflicts, and what does not hold.

What does it cost and how fast is it?

Fixed price CHF 790. Delivery within 48 hours of written confirmation and a complete target-company brief. No subscription, no extra hours. The enquiry itself is not yet a paid order — we confirm scope and delivery date before any cost arises.

Can I not just google this myself?

You do not get a link dump. You get a prioritised working base: contradictions ordered, sources traceable, risky claims marked for the first meeting.

What if no material contradictions are found?

You still receive the full claim log with reviewed statements, sources and marks. A quiet finding is still a usable result.

When does the delivery clock start?

Delivery is within 48 hours of written confirmation and a complete target-company brief. Weekend confirmations start the clock on Monday at 08:00.

Do you need confidential papers?

No. We work only with public sources. There are no uploads. Confidential documents are neither accepted nor processed.

Is the Clash File an expert opinion or a due diligence?

Neither. It is a structured record of public statements with source and retrieval date — no opinion, no verdict, no substitute for due diligence.
