5 · Frequently asked questions

Short answers.

What exactly do I get?

A clear research question, 5–12 reliable sources with a link and a short note on each, the key findings with the exact supporting passage, and a list of what is not supported. Everything is separated into supported, plausible and open. Sources and dossier can be in German or English.

What does it cost?

A one-off CHF 190 to 390. Mini from CHF 190 covers a tightly defined question and about 5 sources; Standard up to CHF 390 covers 8–12 sources. You receive the fixed price in writing before any work starts. VAT only where due.

How quickly is it ready?

5–7 working days after written confirmation of the order. First we clarify the question and the deadline, then the work begins. We decline jobs with a deadline less than 5 working days away.

What do you need from me?

Your question in one sentence and your deadline. If it helps, also the language, the use (book or episode) and any sources you already know. Sources and dossier can be in German or English.

Do you also write the text?

No. No script, no ghostwriting, no interviews and no legal or medical sign-off. You get the source work; your voice stays yours.

Does the enquiry cost anything?

No. The enquiry is non-binding. After the written fixed price and order confirmation, you pay in advance via a Stripe link. Only then does the work begin. After delivery there is one round of corrections within 48 hours.
